What is Howloween?

Howloween is an annual Halloween-themed mini convention at UC San Diego, hosted by Triton Tails, UCSD's resident furry club. Attendees can enjoy a wide range of activities, from vendor fairs and gaming to costume and dance competitions, night raves, and more!

Howloween started in 2024 with just an artist alley and a couple of rooms. Since then, it has grown into a much larger event, bringing in many different student orgs on campus for collaboration and hundreds of attendees each year.
