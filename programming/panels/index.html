Panels

Panels are free to be hosted by the community! Whether its a meetup to meet and talk to others with similar interestes, a workshop to showcase how to craft, or even a panel to have fun, maybe learn something new, or even play some games! Panels will be spread out throughout the con with even more opportunity to make your Howloween experience ever more unique.
