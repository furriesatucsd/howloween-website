DoodleDrawz

🎨 Doodle Drawz is a proud advocate for those with chronic illness/disabilities. Having one himself, Doodle always looks for opportunities to advocate for representation for those with chronic illnesses or disabilities in the fandom. He’s also a proud member and advocate for the Trans 🏳️‍⚧️ and Asexual 🖤 🩶 🤍 💜 community as well! Doodle is a dedicated fursuit maker of 5 years, and a furry artist of 10+ years, who has won two floor wars competitions 🕺 🪩 🎃 We are SO excited to have them as one of this year's Howloween Special Guests, and you can see them at this year's Vendor Fair, or a Special Sponsor Q&A Panel as well! 🎃
