What's the difference?

UCSD and General Attendees

The UCSD and General tickets are the same in terms of what you get access to at the event! The only differences are the discounted price and requirement of UCSD affiliation for the UCSD ticket. This requires both a valid UCSD email for registration and also some sort of UCSD verification at check-in (i.e. showing your student ID) before picking up your badge.

Sponsors

The Sponsor ticket comes with a few extra perks, including but not limited to:

  • A preorder for our first Triton Tails hoodie at an even cheaper price
  • Opportunities to meet and greet our special guests
  • Free food at the event
  • Supporting the club so we can run Howloween and other huge events yearly :3

Minors

The Minor ticket is largely the same as the UCSD and General tickets, but imposes a few additional restrictions:

  • Additional forms may be required to be signed (will be added here or available at check-in)
  • A parent/guardian must accompany the minor for the entirety of the event (the parent/guardian must also have their own ticket)
  • Minor tickets will not provide access to the Howloween rave or mature programming (if there is any)

More details can be found in the attendee policy as it is made available.
