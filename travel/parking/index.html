Parking

Parking Locations

When parking at UC San Diego, make sure to see if you can find parking at Gilman Parking Structure first (see the image below). If no parking is available there, then refer to this link to look at all other options of parking.

Parking Permits

For this convention, it is highly recommended to buy a D permit for one day as it provides the best balance between convenience and parking fee ($5.25/day). On the day of the convention, a D permit provides access to park in the following spaces:

  • A*, B, S
  • D spaces in lots P703, P704, P705 and P386

*Note that for A spaces, certain parking lots may specify "A permit required 24 hours a day/7 days a week", in which cases a D permit is not valid.

When you park, use either Parkmobile (download for Android or iOS) or buy through the UCSD website to acquire a D 1 day Permit. We strongly recommend buying these permits ahead of time to reduce inconvenience when you arrive.

Perks

UCSD parking comes with a lot of perks:

  • Last for the entire day
  • Have in and out privileges
  • Most parking structures have EV charging stations

Accessibility

If you have an ADA parking permit, you will be using the parking at the ground level for most parking structures within UCSD.
