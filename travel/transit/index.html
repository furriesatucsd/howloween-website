Public Transit

Download the Pronto app first for Android or iOS. There are also physical ticket machines in some stations, but the app is by far the most convenient and accessible option. If you are a San Diego local, you can check if you have any discounts for transit in the Pronto app. Along with the information on this page, also refer to Google Maps and Apple maps.


Trolley

Price for one-way: $3.00

The trolley is by far the most convenient route towards campus as one of the stops is directly on campus, only a short walk from Price Center. If you are near a trolley station in San Diego, you will eventually be able to get onto the Blue Line, where the trolley can take you to UC San Diego Central Campus.


Bus

Price for one-way: $3.00

The buses that directly stop at UC San Diego are the:

  • Super Loops (201 and 202)
  • 30
  • 41
  • 921
  • NCTD 101

Amtrak/Coaster

Price and schedule vary, so look at their respective website for more information:

  • Amtrack
  • Coaster

The stop for either transportation mode should be at Old Town or at Santa Fe Depot. Afterwards, you can take the Blue Line trolley north to UC San Diego. Amtrak and Coaster may have discounts for students, kids, elderly, veterans, and more. They also have occasional promotional discounts year round, so keep an eye out for that as well!
